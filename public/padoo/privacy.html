Padoo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Padoo lets an iPhone or iPad act as a trackpad, air pointer, and app deck for a computer. It consists of an iPhone/iPad app and a companion computer app.

In short

Padoo has no accounts and no sign-in, and it never tracks you across apps or websites. What you do with the trackpad, what you type and what is on your computer stay between your own devices.

To find out where Padoo works and where it gets stuck, the iPhone/iPad app sends anonymous usage statistics. You can turn this off at any time.

Anonymous usage statistics

The iPhone/iPad app sends short, anonymous events to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service based in Germany that stores data in the EU. Examples: "the first computer was found", "connected over the cable", "the unlock screen was shown", "Padoo crashed".

An event can include:

  • which step was reached (for example: pairing started, pairing succeeded),
  • how a connection was made or why it failed, as a category such as "timed out", never an error message,
  • how long connecting or a session took, and which Padoo features were used in it (trackpad, air pointer, keyboard, mic typing, dock, deck, sliders),
  • crash and freeze counts, with the crash type number only,
  • purchase steps (unlock screen shown, purchase completed or cancelled) and, for a completed purchase, its price and currency,
  • the app version, the device model, the system version, and your language and region settings.

An event never includes your name, your Apple Account, your email, your computer's name, the names of your apps, anything you type or say, your location. TelemetryDeck does not store IP addresses.

Events are tied to a random identifier that is hashed on your device and again on TelemetryDeck's servers before anything is stored, so it cannot be traced back to you or your device. Nothing is shared with advertisers or data brokers, and nothing is combined with data from other apps.

Turning it off: on the "Computers on this network" screen, tap the "⋯" menu and turn off Share Anonymous Usage. From then on nothing is sent, not even a note that you turned it off.

Report a Problem

The same "⋯" menu has Report a Problem. It opens an email to us with Padoo's log from your iPhone or iPad attached, plus any recent crash reports. The log lists computer names, the names of apps in front on your computer, and connection steps, never what you type. You see the whole email first, you can delete anything from it, and nothing is sent unless you tap Send. We use what you send only to answer you and fix the problem, and we delete it when it is no longer needed for that.

What travels between your devices

Apart from the anonymous statistics above, Padoo's network traffic is between your iPhone or iPad and your computer, on your own local network or over a cable. That traffic carries:

  • Your input: finger positions, motion-derived cursor positions, and button presses, sent so your Mac can act on them.
  • The icons and names of the apps in your Mac's Dock, sent to the phone so the key deck can show them.
  • The name of the app currently in front on your Mac, sent so the phone can offer the right buttons for it, such as Back in a browser or Step Over in a debugger. The name only ever travels to your own phone, and nothing about what you are doing inside that app is read or sent.
  • Your Mac's volume and brightness levels, so the phone's sliders can show where they are set.
  • Timing information used to measure and correct latency.

The receiving device uses this information in real time and does not retain it. None of it is sent to us or anywhere outside your paired devices.

What stays on your devices

  • Pairing keys are generated on your devices and stored in the system keychain. They never leave.
  • Your settings (gesture thresholds, connection preference, deck layout, calibration) are stored locally on the device they belong to.
  • A diagnostic log is written locally to help troubleshoot a connection. It stays on your device unless you send it with Report a Problem. You can delete it at any time.

Permissions, and why

  • Local Network (iPhone): to find your Mac and send input to it. Used for nothing else.
  • Camera (iPhone): used only to scan the pairing code your Mac displays. No photo is taken, stored, or transmitted.
  • Motion sensors (iPhone): used while the air pointer is active to work out where you are aiming, and while the deck is visible to move the light across its glass keys.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition (iPhone/iPad): used only while you choose mic-only typing. Speech is turned into text on the device, and only the text goes to your computer.
  • Bluetooth (iPhone and Mac): used only when you choose the Bluetooth connection or turn on DuoLink, to send input straight from your iPhone to your Mac. It goes directly between your own devices, encrypted, and nowhere else.
  • Accessibility (Mac): required for the Mac app to move your cursor and press your keys. This is the app's core function and the only thing the permission is used for.

Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data a company holds about you. The usage statistics are anonymous, so we cannot pick out yours, but you can stop them at any time with the switch above. If you have emailed us, ask and we will delete your messages. Deleting the apps removes everything they stored on your devices.

Children

Padoo is rated 4+. The anonymous statistics are the same for everyone and identify no one, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. Until version 1.6.2 Padoo sent no usage statistics at all.
