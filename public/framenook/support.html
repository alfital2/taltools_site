What you need

  • An iPhone or iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later
  • For another screen: both devices on the same local Wi-Fi network
  • A modern browser on the TV, tablet, phone, or computer you want to use

Opening your first screen

  1. Add one or more photos or videos in Library.
  2. Open Screens.
  3. On another device connected to the same Wi-Fi, scan the QR code or type the address FrameNook shows.
  4. Keep FrameNook open while that browser downloads its local copy of the show.

You can also choose Play on This Device to run the same player directly on your iPhone or iPad.

Common questions

The browser cannot open the address.

Keep FrameNook open and make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi. On the iPhone or iPad, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and make sure FrameNook is allowed.

The screen stopped receiving changes.

FrameNook needs to remain open while a browser downloads new media or settings. Reopen the app, then refresh the display. After a compatible browser finishes saving its copy, it can keep playing without the phone or tablet.

A photo from iCloud will not import.

Open Photos and make sure the original can finish downloading, then try adding it again. FrameNook asks Apple’s Photos framework for the item you selected.

Why is the weather missing?

Turn Weather on in Settings and choose a town or city. FrameNook uses Apple Maps to find the place and Apple Weather for the forecast. The display includes Apple’s required attribution.

How do I remove my media?

Delete items from the FrameNook Library. This removes FrameNook’s local copies; it does not delete originals from Photos or Files.

Does FrameNook upload my photos?

No. Media is stored on your device and, when you open a screen, transferred over your local network to that browser. See the privacy policy for details.
