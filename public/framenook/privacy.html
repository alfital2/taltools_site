Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

FrameNook turns an iPhone or iPad into a private hub for photo and video displays on the same local network.

The developer collects no data

FrameNook does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data with the developer. There is no FrameNook account, analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party crash-reporting service.

Your photos, videos, settings, and display list are not uploaded to the developer.

What stays on your device

  • Photos and videos you choose, plus copies FrameNook prepares for playback
  • Slideshow settings, screen names, crops, ordering, and saved place names
  • The local address and configuration used to serve your displays

Deleting media from FrameNook removes its local copy. It does not delete the original from Photos or Files.

What travels on your local network

When you open a FrameNook address on another device, the app sends the player, your slideshow settings, and your selected media directly to that browser over your local Wi-Fi network. Compatible browsers may store a local copy so the display can keep playing. The developer does not receive this traffic.

Apple services

Some optional features communicate with Apple:

  • Photos and iCloud Photos: Apple’s Photos framework provides only the items you select. If an original is stored in iCloud, Apple may download it to your device.
  • Place names: coordinates already embedded in selected media may be sent to Apple’s geocoding service to turn them into a readable place name.
  • Weather: a town or city you search for is resolved through Apple Maps, and its coordinates are sent to Apple Weather for the forecast.

Apple handles this information under Apple’s Privacy Policy. It is not sent to the FrameNook developer.

Permissions

  • Photos: to import the photos and videos you choose.
  • Local Network: to make your slideshow available to screens on the same Wi-Fi network.

Children

FrameNook collects no data from anyone, including children. The app does not contain advertising, social features, messaging, or broadly distributed user-generated content.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
